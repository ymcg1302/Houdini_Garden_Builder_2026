## Ideation and Initial Pipeline

My initial idea was to create a Garden Season Changer as shown below based on the project brief [here](https://brightspace.bournemouth.ac.uk/d2l/le/lessons/675123/topics/2488367). It would include the following: 
* A simple diorama setup where the focus of the scene was a bench setup in the middle of a small grassy mound with bushes, trees, and a hanging or background element such as trees or a trellis with vines.
* A Houdini tool or mechanism focused on changing the color and removing or generating foliage depending on the season:
  * <ins>Summer/Spring</ins> = featuring green flowering bushes, a warm-colored, wooden bench, and a white-wood trellis full of vines, ivy, and flowers
  * <ins>Fall</ins> = a sparser landscape with yellow, orange, and brown colored hues for the grass, vines, including fallen leaves from the vines, trees, and bushes
  * <ins>Winter</ins> = featuring an almost barren setting with stick-like branches and bushes and an empty trellis cover with white powder signifying snow

# After Initial Feedback

The idea is much more simplified. Honing in on the _Alice in Wonderland_ or _chess_ themed aspect of the project, this allowed me to break the project up into 3 elements (compared to the 3 mini projects of the previous idea). While the previous idea was good, I was asked to consider an idea that focused on building the garden instead of altering a finished one, like a _Garden Grower_ or a _Hedge Builder_ both of which are equally fantastic ideas. For the sake of simplicity and posterity, the new idea can be broken up into 3 parts based on the mood board below:
* Hedges shaped either like chess pieces or the desired card suits (examples [here] and [here].
* A checkboard garden floor with alternating between grass and stone (could potentially be made with a version of an Alpha Map).
* Roses either on the hedges, borders, or in between = this may be either a _Stretch Goal_ or a _Decorative Element_ as this is more about generation and propagation while the other two aspects of the project are more setup and structure focused.
